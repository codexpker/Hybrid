\subsection{A. Protocol and reproduction
details}\label{a.-protocol-and-reproduction-details}

All intervals use half-open indexing. The SWaT timeline concatenates the
495,000-point source prefix and the published 449,919-point test
segment. In PSM, target positions are relative to the released
87,841-point test record. The PSM source prefix contains 132,481 points.
Source scaler statistics for the primary LEFT detector follow its native
protocol over the entire released source prefix, including its
validation interval. The source-only and replay draws are restricted to
SOURCE\_FIT. The model's adaptation input is limited to legal complete
windows from the prescribed fit intervals.

The nonoverlapping readout windows have length and step 192. Each
emitted score's availability index is the window's final observed index.
SWaT additionally discards the first 63 legally scored points to retain
the historical common-axis start at 608535, which is inside its first
legal window. The last 63 SWaT and last 97 PSM test points have no
full-window score. The first 152 positions in the PSM raw query role lie
outside complete legal query windows. The final SWaT crop contains
288,544 normal and 47,777 anomalous points, with 22 complete events,
nine of them long. The PSM crop contains 47,309 normal and 17,779
anomalous points, with 48 complete events, five of them long. Two
boundary-truncated PSM events contribute 4,196 of 8,861 and 3,029 of
3,120 points to pointwise metrics and are excluded from the
complete-event analysis. Calibration selects 512 points via NumPy
\texttt{linspace(first,\ last,\ 512,\ dtype=int64)} over legal CAL score
support. Post hoc attack counts are 38 and 512 at the selected SWaT and
PSM locations. The entire PSM CAL role, spanning 3,848 points, is also
attack-labeled.

Each task has one fixed LEFT initialization selected using the native
source validation procedure. The endpoint adaptation budget is 2,000
steps with a new Adam optimizer at learning rate 0.0001 and batch size
256. Sampling is uniform with replacement over legal window starts in
the respective fit interval. Matching draw positions cross arms but
never cross tasks. The original checkpoints include the native
prototypes and running state. In SWaT, noU is cropped from the native
archived scores. In PSM, the source endpoint is restored with its actual
runtime state for the frozen readout. The 2025 ordinary U128 and earlier
restricted-window comparisons remain separate. The implementation and
record give the exact source window roles and seeds.

\href{../../src/hybrid_tsad/left_domain_replay.py}{replay entry point}
uses \texttt{default\_rng(seed)} for \(T\) and
\texttt{SeedSequence(seed).spawn(3)} for \(S\), \(T'\), and \(S'\) in
that order. In the
\href{../../src/hybrid_tsad/left_exposure_bundle.py}{replacement
generator}, \texttt{SeedSequence({[}seed,\ 20261118,\ 10{]}).spawn(3)}
supplies separate streams for slots, abnormal donors, and nominally
normal donors. Slots are drawn without replacement and donors with
replacement. Original numeric arrays, weights, optimizers, and complete
score banks reside in the separate experiment archive. Public files
contain code, fixed configurations, asset fingerprints, and derived
tables. Recreating the numeric input tensors and restoring the archived
artifacts require those assets.

The range metrics use the archived LEFT
\texttt{metricor.RangeAUC\_volume\_opt} path with
\texttt{windowSize=100} and \texttt{thre=250}. For AP and ROC-AUC the
full scored axis retains the original labels without point adjustment.
Strict and three-point confirmed streams are evaluated independently.
Any-hit measures episode overlap, whereas the chronological greedy
one-to-one rule limits each alarm fragment and each event to one match.
Normal-region fragments count contiguous alarm portions within normal
labels and can include fragments clipped at anomaly boundaries. Long
steady macro coverage averages the coverage within each eligible event
after its first 512 samples. Pooled steady coverage weights events by
their steady durations. All reported offsets are in sample positions.
Full-window availability and hit-only medians limit their use as latency
comparisons.

\subsection{C. Expanded external
comparisons}\label{c.-expanded-external-comparisons}

The local MLP and CANDI comparisons share point indices with the main
tasks but use 10-point endpoint scores and their own fitted source
scalers and calibration scores. The MLP noU, TT-A, and R-A results
separate source initialization and learning policy within that backbone.
Full CANDI uses its own lightweight adaptation and is frozen after its
authorized U-FIT stream. Its normal reference and internal screening
thresholds derive from source validation. Its external alarm threshold
derives from the project CAL points. The
\href{../../docs/experiments/mlp_replay_20261119/RESULTS.md}{cross-backbone
comparison},
\href{../../docs/experiments/mlp_replay_20261119/comparison.csv}{full
table}, and
\href{../../docs/experiments/mlp_replay_20261119/curves/SWAT_PR_ROC.png}{two-task
curves} preserve seed 2025 and the scores' own CAL operating points. See
also the separate
\href{../../docs/experiments/mlp_replay_20261119/curves/PSM_PR_ROC.png}{PSM
curves} and
\href{../../docs/experiments/candi_native_a_20261118/RESULTS.md}{CANDI
permissions and costs}.

The
\href{../../docs/experiments/timeradar_native_v_replay_20261120/RESULTS.md}{separate
native TimeRadar comparison} preserves the official initial weights, its
100-point context and variance readout, and the distinct PSM query
endpoint axis. It uses its own fixed thresholds.
\href{../../docs/experiments/timercd_fixed_20261117/logit/RESULTS.md}{TimeRCD}
is also archived with distinct source training, normalization, and
context permissions. The \href{../../docs/research_scope.md}{TSAD-C
compatibility record} documents the published interval-score versus
project point-score mismatch and local NA. No published headline number
fills an unavailable local result.
