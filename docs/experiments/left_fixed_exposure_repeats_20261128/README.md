# Fixed-plan LEFT adaptation repeats

The [frozen protocol](PROTOCOL.md) compares ordinary replay (R) and its existing window-weighted counterpart (W) on PSM and SWaT. The archived 2025 reference uses the same model parents, windows and researcher-defined exposure plans as the 2026 and 2027 runs. The random state of native adaptation changes across these trajectories. Both domains have prior development exposure. All three adaptations within a domain share one source model and each condition's fixed plan.

- [Identity binding](identity.json) ties each cell to the source checkpoint, scaler, archived teacher bank, plans, weights and 2025 checkpoint hashes. The real-batch probes for [PSM](rng_probe_PSM.json) and [SWaT](rng_probe_SWAT.json) establish effective stochasticity. Both probes change dropout activations and the updated model state when changing the training seed.
- [Execution and bank receipts](receipts.json) bind each new checkpoint to its newly scored QUERY bank, including the four early paired batch/RNG fingerprints, fixed 2,000-step exposure, checkpoint and bank hashes, score axes and device timings.
- [Complete new metrics](new_metrics.csv) include all four ranking measures, QUERY ranges and the AP/ROC-AUC recomputation error. The fixed VUS implementation is recorded in the [environment receipt](evaluation_versions.txt).
- [All 36 endpoints](endpoints.csv) include the 12 archived references. [Paired differences](paired.csv) include absolute scores and within-seed contrasts for each metric. [Summary statistics](paired_summary.csv) give means, sample SD, range and positive, zero and negative counts. See the [original exposure controls](../left_evidence_freeze_20261128/abc_metrics.csv) for replay alternatives outside the repeated matrix.

## Interpretation

All three AP interactions are positive in both domains. PSM has a mean interaction of +0.012030 with sample SD 0.006904 and range [+0.004058, +0.016132]. SWaT has a mean of +0.003415 with sample SD 0.000577 and range [+0.002752, +0.003808]. W also exceeds R in absolute B AP in all runs. Its mean B advantage is +0.019211 on PSM and +0.004056 on SWaT. The mean natural AP advantage is +0.005228 on PSM and +0.000850 on SWaT. SWaT loses mean VUS-ROC under B and both mean VUS measures under A. This supports conditional stability of direction for the fixed exposure response. The magnitude, task transfer and operational behavior remain limited by the measured protocols. See [all absolute and relative metrics](paired.csv).

The B−C interaction compares whole policies under this matched replacement. Donor operating conditions can differ even when slot locations and counts match. Window weights are ranks of each full condition's target plan, so unchanged windows can have different weights across conditions. The three trajectories do not measure variability across alternative exposure plans or pretraining. SMD28 retains its original 28-machine, single-seed evaluation. Operating metrics in the manuscript are from the archived single run. PSM and SWaT CAL do not establish a normal 1% false-positive rate.

## Execution and reproduction

All 24 endpoints and 24 QUERY banks completed. [The cost ledger](costs.json) accounts for 0.559 A40 hours including probes and lane overhead, with no failed GPU work. Four independent lanes completed within 8.53 minutes. The score contract is [22656,87744) on PSM and [608535,944856) on SWaT, with identical archived score availability at block end. The four-metric CPU evaluation took 108.96 seconds. Every new AP/ROC pair was reproduced by a second implementation, with maximum error 2.22×10⁻¹⁶. VUS was evaluated with one pinned implementation. Source, calibration and SMD results retain their archived scope.

The new checkpoints and banks were copied to durable author storage and independently checked against the recorded SHA-256 identities. The repository contains the statistics, identity receipts, source code and analysis tools. The [reproducibility guide](../../../manuscript/next_generation_20261115/REPRODUCIBILITY.md) describes the author-archive permissions and the exact inputs needed for scoring or refitting. The three figures and full [paper](../../../manuscript/next_generation_20261115/draft.md) use these versioned tables.
