# Fixed F comparison of three detector initializations

This is a single-seed public-support adaptation comparison on the existing PSM and SMD machine-3-2 tasks. Three existing Z checkpoints supply the official TimeRadar initialization, normally reconstructed source continuation, and point-weighted real-source-event supervision. Each receives the identical target support plan and fixed 240-update F rule. The official initialization plus matched F is a project control.

The support label budget per task is one complete abnormal event plus 512 normal observations. PSM uses the event [3248,3439) and normal block [92,604); SMD machine-3-2 uses [3080,3917) and [161,673). They provide 92/738 legal positive and 413 legal normal endpoints respectively. All 100 observations of every training window stay within their own support block. The learner receives the two arrays and the sealed draw plan. Public truth is used to construct the support set. SMD machine-3-3 has no eligible complete-window support event and remains NA.

Each update has 32 normal-copy windows, 15 positive endpoints (five from each of three time strata) and 15 paired normal endpoints. The pair uses log1p(V/a_F), softplus margin one, coefficient one, and legal-positive-count weights. The normal loss uses all positions, variables and ten copies. Across 240 updates the exposures are 7,680 normal reconstruction windows plus 3,600 positive and 3,600 matched normal windows. Repeated exposures are drawn from the same one event. The common task-specific a_F is the official initialization's median V over all 413 normal support endpoints, protected below by 1e-8.

Only the native decoder updates, with fresh AdamW under the existing source-training settings and 24-update warmup. Each native input is one 100-point window with ten fixed complementary masks in eval mode. The deployed score remains raw native endpoint V, with sample variance and the original scaler. Step 240 supplies the endpoint; the Z row is the separately archived pre-adaptation reference.

PSM ranking and operation both use the fixed 64,728-point suffix for this experiment. The historical full-test ranking table retains its separate axis. All 512 PSM CAL points have attack labels. SMD machine-3-2 uses 14,746 QUERY points and its nominal-normal CAL. Each method uses its own CAL V, q99/higher and strict exceedance. The normal support block is excluded from CAL. Three-confirmation starts at the third consecutive crossing without backfill. Event matching and any-hit are recorded separately. No long event qualifies in the SMD suffix.

NASA generalization and SMD28 historical tests remain archived. This study measures target-label-supported F and its dependence on initialization. Its conclusions apply to one fixed support event and one training seed in each task.

## Fixed result and technical decision

[Complete six-before/six-after method table and paired changes](TABLES.md) · [full precision metrics](methods.csv) · [paired values](paired.csv) · [event details](events.csv)

In PSM, the supervised-V initialization had the strongest pre-F AP, 0.613415. Following the common F rule its AP fell to 0.534156, below official-initialization F (0.547156) and reconstruction-continuation F (0.543705). Candidate F minus its Z was −0.079259 AP; candidate F minus copy F was −0.009549; candidate F minus official F was −0.012999. ROC-AUC and both VUS measures were also lower than either F control. Three-confirmation candidate recall rose from 0.373328 to 0.631150, while observed normal FPR rose from 0.059185 to 0.366061. Its one-to-one event matches fell from 10 to 7 of 48, while any-hit rose from 11 to 22 and normal fragments fell from 93 to 75. Macro and pooled long steady coverage rose to 1.0; the large false-alarm point rate accompanies this gain. At F, all three initializations matched seven events with any-hit 22. Their normal FPRs were approximately 37%, constrained by the unchanged attack-only CAL.

For SMD machine-3-2, official, copy and supervised-V Z AP were 0.036035, 0.034844 and 0.031738. The corresponding F AP values were 0.027201, 0.026936 and 0.026687. Candidate F minus Z was −0.005051; minus copy F −0.000249; minus official F −0.000514. All three F detectors improved ROC-AUC and VUS over their own Z states, yet each had zero QUERY abnormal recall and zero hits among eight complete events at the fixed CAL threshold. Candidate F three-confirmation normal FPR was 0.000069, with one false fragment. The longest event miss remained 123 points; long steady coverage is NA. Threshold-independent ranking and this operating outcome describe distinct effects.

**Decision.** This fixed one-event F rule did not establish a more useful supervised-V initialization. Candidate F had lower AP than both F controls on both tasks. PSM also lost its initial advantage in ROC and VUS; SMD retained metric tradeoffs with no operational event discovery. Retain the previous source-supervised public-ranking and V/E evidence within their tested scopes. The stopped U recipe and this F result supply no basis to claim reliable target adaptation or contamination robustness. F here uses one support event and one seed per task, and includes normal-support labels as an explicit resource. No threshold or endpoint selection was made from QUERY outcomes.

## Compute and independent check

Six training runs, including the common-scale checks, consumed 1,517.09 occupied A40-seconds (0.4214 GPU-hours). Necessary CAL and QUERY readouts consumed 1,901.81 A40-seconds (0.5283 GPU-hours). Total recorded cost was 0.9497 A40-hours. PSM Z scores were reused from the compatible banks. SMD's historical full-axis scaler differed from the current mixed-task scaler, so the required three Z columns were scored alongside F columns with the fixed task input. Score receipts retain the actual weights, scaler, indices, window starts, availability times and cache SHA. Public support exposure consisted of 7,680 copy plus 3,600 positive and 3,600 pair-normal windows per run; this does not enlarge the event-label budget.

The single independent CPU review verified the original support arrays, event labels, earliest normal blocks, sampling footprints, six fixed-endpoint checkpoint identities and shared plans/scales. It independently reproduced PSM three-F AP/ROC, CAL thresholds, strict/three-confirmation point counts, normal fragments and any-hit with zero discrepancy.
