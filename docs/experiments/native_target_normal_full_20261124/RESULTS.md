# Full-parameter versus decoder-only target-normal TimeRadar

[Complete method and scope tables](TABLES.md) · [Entity metrics](entity_metrics.csv) · [Entity paired distributions](paired_distribution.csv) · [Independent recomputation](review.json)

Six full-parameter runs start from the original official weights, the archived seed2025 source-reconstruction step120 or the all-position source-supervised step200. All six execute 20 epochs and select epoch20 by the frozen earliest-minimum normal-validation criterion over epoch0 through epoch20. PSM uses 680 updates and 21,180 window presentations per run. MSL uses 300 updates and 9,080 presentations per run. The previous target-normal plan and scaler files are read in place by SHA. Independent B1 forwards hold the ten complementary masks, eval mode and the per-copy reconstruction loss fixed. The experiment includes all native learnable parameters in AdamW. Source replay and supervised pair losses are outside this target-normal program.

The six real-window checks give exact loss and V agreement with decoder-only initializations before training. After one actual update, every native group has nonzero gradient and parameter movement. Training preserves the three native buffers. Each frozen model computes its own fractional order and encoder output during scoring. The 28 score banks retain the original full-window input footprint and window-end availability. Both tasks use project seed2025 and the previously archived common offline axes (87,645 PSM points and 69,495 MSL points). MSL averages entity metrics with equal weight over all 27 sensor-0 records. The three initializations and target-normal data, scaler, permutation plans and update counts are shared across the two trainable scopes. Zero-update rows inherit the same scaler. The source reconstruction initialization historically used public source-event labels for checkpoint selection. The all-position initialization additionally received a source pair gradient. Neither target training scope uses target anomaly labels. The frozen external scores preserve their original permissions: LEFT uses target-normal training with W192 on PSM and W96 on MSL, CATCH uses target FIT-only statistics and a full W192 context, and PCA uses target FIT-only statistics and the observation at the scored time. CATCH seeds remain separate. No compatible independent CAL operation metrics are asserted. The historical PSM CAL consists of attack labels, and the MSL normal scaler uses the original validation/CAL part of the normal arrays.

Full-parameter training improves AP, ROC-AUC and both VUS metrics over decoder-only training for every matched initialization in both tasks. The AP increments for official, source-reconstruction and all-position-supervised initializations are +0.002471, +0.006010 and +0.003547 on PSM, and +0.102551, +0.075095 and +0.059991 on MSL. On MSL, those scope contrasts have 17/10, 17/10 and 16/11 entity AP wins/losses with respective median changes +0.032369, +0.023717 and +0.013921. This is a change in target training scope. Normal reconstruction MSE is also lower in all six matched contrasts.

The all-position source-supervised initialization does not yield an additional AP benefit under full-parameter training. Against source reconstruction, its AP differences are -0.000894 (PSM) and -0.011047 (MSL). Against the official initialization they are -0.001057 and -0.032479. On PSM the supervised initialization is lower on all four metrics than both other full-parameter arms. On MSL it improves ROC-AUC and VUS-ROC slightly over both but loses AP and VUS-PR. Its MSL AP distribution is 12 wins/15 losses with median -0.000775 against source reconstruction, and 17 wins/10 losses with median +0.001302 against official initialization despite a lower macro mean. Thus the macro loss against official initialization does not describe every entity.

The best trained TimeRadar AP remains below complete LEFT by 0.150750 on PSM and 0.043896 on MSL. The best trained PSM AP also remains below PCA by 0.108292 and below the source-supervised zero-update reference by 0.012735. TimeRadar full-parameter training reduces all four gaps to LEFT within each initialization, especially on MSL, where the official initialization reaches AP 0.464447 versus LEFT 0.508343. The MSL gain belongs to the broader target training scope, with the strongest AP obtained from the official initialization. This single-seed result closes the current sequence of local source-supervised implementation revisions. Its bounded source-only and earlier decoder-only evidence remains in the corresponding panels.

Training used 3303.697 recorded A40-seconds versus 1930.744 for the decoder-only runs (1.711x). Independent full-parameter readout used 63.996 A40-seconds. Combined recorded training and readout cost is 0.935470 GPU-hours. The preflight logical batches took 6.851 additional seconds, with setup outside that measurement. The detailed cost ledger records CPU evaluation time separately.

The independent CPU check verified all 126 epoch states and 126 normal-validation banks, the six original initializations, optimizer exposure counts, parameter updates and selection. Raw AP and ROC were recomputed for all 28 objects, three new methods and two axes (168 rows), with zero difference. VUS review covers aggregation, paired differences and entity distributions. The six selected states, 126 validation banks and 28 score banks were downloaded and hash verified under the workspace results directory. Full epoch checkpoints remain on the research server.
