## Frozen method and nearest work

Let the frozen detector be the width-specific native LEFT parent. Let `a[b,i,t]` be the channel-mean native point score on the actual i-th target presentation among the 16 target slots at update b. In each task or exposure condition, the procedure ranks all `M = 2000 × 16 × 192` scores together, including repeated presentations. If ties receive their average one-based rank, then

```text
r[b,i,t] = (midrank(a[b,i,t]) − 0.5) / M
u[b,i,t] = 1.5 − r[b,i,t]
w[b,i]   = mean_t(u[b,i,t]) / mean_(i,t)(u[b,i,t])
```

Here `w[b,i]` has mean one over the target half of each batch. Source weights are one. Window weighting applies the scalar to each example's native loss reductions, preserving original scale and coefficient reductions. Formally, for per-window native data term `ell` and independent native terms `Omega`, the weighted reduction is `(sum_i w[b,i] ell(T[b,i]) + sum_j ell(S[b,j])) / 32 + Omega`. The unweighted replay reference sets `w=1`. The local variant uses the underlying point weights on the W192 grid and window means for coarse/spectral terms. This is a weighting of loss reductions, and the entire original batch is still read by native LEFT's forward and prototype machinery. The coefficients are frozen for fitting, and the student is deployed with the native score and no weighting. ## What the evidence supports

- **Natural target learning.** Relative to replay, weighting changes AP by +0.002909 on PSM, +0.001063 on SWaT, and −0.001477 in the SMD28 macro average. SWaT loses both VUS metrics relative to replay and remains below frozen Z in AP. On SMD28, 12 machines gain and 16 lose AP. Mean ROC-AUC and VUS-PR decline while VUS-ROC gains +0.000488. This rules out a general improvement claim on the evaluated suite.
- **Matched abnormal replacement.** Within each task, the exposure intervention substitutes the same 3,200 shared slots. The B−C AP loss of ordinary replay is −0.020624 on PSM and −0.005484 on SWaT. For window weighting it is −0.004491 and −0.002732. The corresponding interactions are +0.016132 and +0.002752, with absolute B gains +0.020367 and +0.003856. SWaT's absolute B VUS-ROC still falls by 0.001231. This supports attenuation of the particular AP penalty under the fixed source parents, seed, donor pools and replacement count. B/C match slot and count, while donors may differ in their normal operating context. Each condition also recomputes ranks over its own complete exposure, so an interaction measures the whole policy, including changed weights for untouched inputs. It does not separately identify a direct effect of edited slots.
- **Specificity controls.** Correctly assigned weights beat the single within-batch permutation in PSM B by 0.017951 AP at identical weight mass and multiset. Local weighting improves PSM natural AP by 0.002886 over window weighting, then gives back 0.001481 under B. Explicit EMA had no writes in the traced matrix. Source constraints delivered very small incremental AP changes. These are measured limits to the candidate explanations, and detection results are reported with operational costs.
- **What replication would have addressed.** Only seed2025 supports the core comparison. Stability would require fresh matched replay/weight trajectories with the same frozen source identities, per-seed shared target/source plans, equal budgets, one fixed readout, both original domains and all 28 SMD machines. A separate claim about the B−C effect would need multiple paired B/C draws and seeds, with normal donor context addressed and test-axis fixed. A claim about frozen-versus-dynamic weighting would need a same-backbone control, beyond the existing permutation control. These are evidence gaps, not scheduled experiments. Machine-level spread describes one set of training runs, not independent random-seed replications.
